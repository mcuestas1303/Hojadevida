## Paquete

- **Título recomendado:** Wave Hello: Hello, Farm with Tiky and Lumi (42 caracteres)
- **Otras opciones:** Wave Hello (Ages 18–24 Months): Hello, Farm (43 caracteres); Wave Hello: Tiky and Lumi on the Farm (37 caracteres)
- **Descripción:**

```text
Your child practices waving hello when Tiky and Lumi wave to them.
For toddlers 18 to 24 months, to watch together with a grown-up.

In this episode, Tiky the chick and Lumi the cow say hello with a wing and a hoof. They move slowly and wait a moment for your child to answer. If your child doesn't wave, that's okay: they wave back anyway.

For grown-ups: Wave together and say hello slowly. The next time someone arrives, wave and say "hello," then wait, without asking your child to do it. When someone leaves, wave and say "bye-bye."
After the video: find a stuffed animal or a toy at home and wave hello to it. Then wave hello to someone in the family.

Episode 1 of Tiky and Lumi on the Farm. It runs 1 minute 28 seconds. There is no music during the questions, so your child can hear the voice.
```
