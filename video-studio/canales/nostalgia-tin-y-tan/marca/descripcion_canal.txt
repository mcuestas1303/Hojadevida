DESCRIPCIÓN DEL CANAL (inglés, EE. UU.; se pega tal cual en YouTube Studio → Personalización → Perfil → Descripción)
Tin & Tan: nostalgia, calculated.

Tan remembers. Tin does the math.

Remember rewinding a cassette with a pencil? We answer the little questions you grew up with, using real numbers, step by step, in short videos made for anyone who grew up in the 70s, 80s and 90s.

Every number comes with its source and its assumptions in the description.

The drawings, music and sound effects are original, made with code. The voices are AI-generated.
